## 🔎 A question I've been working on

### [What can a company's connections tell us about fraud? ↗](https://github.com/RookieZhou0617/risk-aware-financial-fraud-detection)

Financial data and annual reports already tell us a lot. My master's thesis asks what corporate relations can add once we understand a company's own signals. I combine financial data, text and company history, then explore the extra information carried by a graph.

The repo shares the method, results and uncertainty, plus a small synthetic PyTorch demo. The graph helps in some settings and hurts in others; those mixed results are part of the story.

[Explore the project](https://github.com/RookieZhou0617/risk-aware-financial-fraud-detection) · [Try the demo](https://github.com/RookieZhou0617/risk-aware-financial-fraud-detection/tree/main/examples) · [Results & limitations](https://github.com/RookieZhou0617/risk-aware-financial-fraud-detection/blob/main/docs/experiments.md)

This interest goes back to my undergraduate studies at the **University of Jinan**, where I explored telecom fraud detection with graph neural networks and ensemble learning. Class imbalance and fraud detection have followed me from one thesis to the next.
